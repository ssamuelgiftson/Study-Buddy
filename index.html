📌 Key Formulas - Grade VIII

Algebra

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

(a - b)² = a² - 2ab + b²

(a + b)(a - b) = a² - b²

(a + b)³ = a³ + 3a²b + 3ab² + b³

Mensuration

Area of Trapezium = ½ × (a + b) × h

Area of Rhombus = ½ × d₁ × d₂

Surface Area of Cube = 6a²

Volume of Cube = a³

Geometry

Sum of angles in triangle = 180°

Sum of angles in quadrilateral = 360°

Exterior angle = Sum of opposite interior angles

Percentage & Profit-Loss

Profit% = (Profit/CP) × 100

Loss% = (Loss/CP) × 100

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

CI = P(1 + R/100)ⁿ - P

📚 Grammar Quick Reference

Parts of Speech

Noun: Name of person, place, thing

Pronoun: Replaces noun (he, she, it)

Verb: Action word (run, eat, write)

Adjective: Describes noun (big, red)

Tenses

Simple Present: He writes daily

Present Cont: He is writing now

Simple Past: He wrote yesterday

Past Cont: He was writing

Active & Passive Voice

Active: Ram eats an apple

Passive: An apple is eaten by Ram

Rule: Object + is/am/are + V3 + by + Subject

Direct & Indirect Speech

Direct: He said, "I am happy."

Indirect: He said that he was happy.

Rule: Change tense, pronoun & remove quotes

Types of Sentences

Declarative: States a fact (.)

Interrogative: Asks a question (?)

Imperative: Gives command (.)

Exclamatory: Shows emotion (!)

Modals

Can: Ability (I can swim)

May: Permission (May I go?)

Must: Necessity (You must study)

Should: Advice (You should rest)

Articles

A: Before consonant sounds

An: Before vowel sounds

The: Specific / already known

Tip: "An hour" (silent h)

Conjunctions

And: Adds information

But: Shows contrast

Because: Shows reason

Although: Despite something

✍️ Writing Tips

📧 Letter Writing

1. Sender's Address (top-right)

2. Date

3. Receiver's Address (left)

4. Subject

5. Salutation (Dear Sir/Madam)

6. Body (3 paragraphs)

7. Closing (Yours faithfully)

📝 Essay Writing

1. Introduction: Hook + Thesis

2. Body 1: Main point + Example

3. Body 2: Supporting point

4. Body 3: Another perspective

5. Conclusion: Summary + Opinion

📊 Notice Writing

1. Name of Organization

2. Title: NOTICE

3. Date

4. Heading

5. Body (What, When, Where, Who)

6. Signature & Designation
